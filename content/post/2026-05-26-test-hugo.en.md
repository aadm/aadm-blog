gannts charts with mermaid? I didnt know!

```mermaid
gantt
    title Project Timeline
    dateFormat  YYYY-MM-DD
    section Setup
        Configure Hugo      :done, setup1, 2026-01-01, 3d
        Install theme       :done, setup2, after setup1, 2d
    section Content
        Write pages         :active, content1, after setup2, 7d

    section Deploy
        Build and test      :deploy1, after content2, 2d

```

another mermaid example:

```mermaid
graph LR
    A[prospect A]
    DRILL((drill))
    S[success]
    F[failure]
    A --> DRILL
    DRILL --> |POS|S
    DRILL --> |1-POS|F

    S --- p90[scenario 1: P90 min]
    S --- p50[scenario 2: P50 mean]
    S --- p10[scenario 3: P10 max]

    p90 -..- Pmin>payoff=10]
    p50 -..- Pmean>payoff=90]
    p10 -..- Pmax>payoff=500]

    F -..- PF>payoff=0]
```
